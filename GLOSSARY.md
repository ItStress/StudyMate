# StudyMate

StudyMate helps learners study PDF documents through grounded answers, explanations, and summaries.

## Language

**Document**:
A PDF supplied as study material and used as a source of evidence.
_Avoid_: File, source PDF

**Citation**:
A reference identifying the document and page supporting an answer, explanation, or summary.
_Avoid_: Reference, source link

**Physical page**:
A page identified by its one-based position in the PDF, regardless of any number printed on the page. It is the canonical page reference for citations.
_Avoid_: Printed page number

**Table**:
Study material arranged in rows and columns whose relationships contribute to its meaning.

**Equation**:
A mathematical expression in study material whose symbols and arrangement contribute to its meaning.

**Diagram**:
A visual representation in study material, such as a process drawing or chart, that may be accompanied by a caption.

**Preparation**:
The conversion of a document into inspectable study content for later retrieval and answering.
_Avoid_: Indexing, embedding

**Reprocessing**:
A new preparation attempt for a previously prepared document.
_Avoid_: Retry, correction
